About

Building the backend of banking.

I've spent the last 12+ years delivering enterprise software across banking, telecom, entertainment and consumer goods — the kind of domains where a bug doesn't just break a page, it breaks a transaction.

Right now I'm leading backend engineering at Backbase, focused on Retail and Commercial Banking, FIS integrations, and onboarding flows for banks across North America and the Middle East & Africa. Before that: full-stack leadership at Cavinkare, and nearly six years across Tech Mahindra and BT building eCommerce platforms for globally distributed teams.

My core stack is Java and Spring Boot, but I move comfortably across ReactJS, Python and the major clouds. What I care about most is clean architecture, event-driven design, and automation that lets teams ship with confidence.
